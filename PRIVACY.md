# Privacy Policy

**harbour-zensors** does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All measurements are read directly from your device — from `/proc` and
`/sys`, from the system daemons over D-Bus, and from the Qt sensor,
positioning and audio APIs. Nothing leaves your phone.

The app:

* has no user accounts and requires no login;
* sends no analytics, telemetry, or crash reports;
* makes no network connections;
* stores nothing outside its own private application data.

The only outbound action is opening a web link (the ko-fi donation page
shown on the About page) in your browser when you tap it. That is a standard
browser hand-off; the app itself does not transmit anything.

If you have questions, contact the maintainer via the repository.
